Ayurvedic Core Principles

Balancing Vatha, Pitha & Kapha

According to Siddha and Ayurveda, health is a state of equilibrium between the three bodily humors. Our treatments address root imbalances rather than just symptoms.

Vatha (Air & Ether)

Controls movement, circulation, and breathing. When unbalanced, it causes joint stiffness, arthritis, dry skin, anxiety, and digestive discomfort. We neutralize Vatha imbalances with warming herbal oil therapies.

Pitha (Fire & Water)

Governs digestion, body temperature, and metabolism. Unbalanced Pitha manifests as inflammatory skin rashes, acidity, chronic allergies, and irritability. We offer cooling therapies to soothe excess heat.

Kapha (Earth & Water)

Governs structure, lubrication, and immune resilience. Excess Kapha leads to congestion, weight gain, asthma, respiratory illnesses, and lethargy. We provide revitalizing herbal remedies to restore balance.
